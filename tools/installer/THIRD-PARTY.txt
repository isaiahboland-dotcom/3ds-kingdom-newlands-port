Programs this package uses besides the project's own:

- AssetRipper 2.0.0 (GPL-3.0), downloaded on first use from
  https://github.com/AssetRipper/AssetRipper/releases and checked against a
  pinned SHA-256. It is not included in this package.
- makerom (Project_CTR, https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR) and
  bannertool (https://github.com/diasurgical/bannertool), built from the pinned
  source revisions named in tools/cia/build.sh, when a .cia is offered.
  Their licences are in those repositories.
